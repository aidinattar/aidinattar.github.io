Over the past few weeks, I've been working on the website for NeuRITES Lab, our research group at the University of Padova, led by Prof. Michele Rossi.
We work on spiking neural networks, neuromorphic computing, and biologically inspired learning, and the new website is a place to bring together what we're doing, the people involved, and our research activities.
You can visit our new website at [neurites.dei.unipd.it](https://neurites.dei.unipd.it/)!
