Bachelor's thesis · University of Padova

Search of gravitational waves signal due to post merger phase of neutron star binary merger

Bachelor’s thesis in Physics focused on the detection and reconstruction of post-merger gravitational-wave signals from binary neutron star systems. The work centers on the Coherent WaveBurst algorithm and on simulated injections in the LIGO-Virgo network, with attention to how well post-coalescence dynamics can be recovered under O4 sensitivity assumptions. The manuscript is written in Italian.
